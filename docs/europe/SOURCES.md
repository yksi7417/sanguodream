The status column says how strong the check is:
- "two sources": two different publishers agree with the life.
- "second source is weak": the second source is a wiki, a genealogy site or a page that copies the English Wikipedia. The officer needs a better source.
- "one source": the research found no second source. The life keeps only the facts that the first source shows.
